Structural optimisation of a can opener

Finite element analysis of a can opener to improve its structural behaviour: an ANSYS APDL model, design optimisation and verification with real tests.

Period
09/2025 – 11/2025
Type
Academic project
My role
Design, simulation and validation
Status
Completed
03REF. 03ANSYS APDL

Tools

  • FEM
  • Optimisation

Goal

Study how the structure of a can opener behaves in use and improve its design based on simulation.

What I did

  • Finite element model of the part in ANSYS APDL.
  • Geometry optimisation based on the results.
  • Experimental validation of the design.
  • Technical documentation of the process and results.
