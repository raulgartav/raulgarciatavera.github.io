Automated colour-sorting machine

A machine that detects the colour of each part with a sensor and sorts it accordingly. It brings mechanics, electronics and programming together in one system. I coordinated the team throughout the project.

Period
01/2026 – 05/2026
Type
Academic team project
My role

Status
Completed
01REF. 01Arduino (C++)

Tools

  • Arduino (C++)
  • SOLIDWORKS
  • 3D printing
  • Stepper motors

The challenge

Design and build an automatic system that recognises parts of different colours and separates them without manual intervention, integrating sensors, actuators and custom mechanisms.

What we did

  • Colour reading of each part with colour sensors.
  • Control program on Arduino, written in C++.
  • Actuation with stepper and DC motors.
  • Mechanisms designed in SOLIDWORKS and 3D-printed.

My role

I coordinated the team: I organised the work among the members and made sure the mechanical design, the electronics and the program came together as one machine.
