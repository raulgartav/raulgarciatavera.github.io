5-DOF robotic arm

A low-cost robotic arm I am building at home with 3D-printed parts, hobby servos and an Arduino UNO. The first goal is for it to pick up objects and place them at given coordinates.

Goal

Build a five-degree-of-freedom arm that, given a target position in coordinates, moves each joint to pick up an object and place it somewhere else. I chose a low-cost route so I can iterate quickly. If it works well, I will upgrade the components.

How it is built

  • Structure made of parts 3D-printed on a Bambu Lab A1.
  • Five joints driven by hobby servos.
  • Controlled by an Arduino UNO.

Next steps

Once the arm places objects reliably, I want to add computer vision so it can locate objects by itself instead of receiving their coordinates.
