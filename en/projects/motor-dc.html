REF. 05 · Practical part of a university assignment

Home-built DC motor

A DC motor built from scratch with two neodymium magnets, three coils and a commutator. The parts are designed in SOLIDWORKS and 3D-printed. It is the practical part of an assignment on electric motors.

Context

The motor accompanies a university assignment on electric motors that starts from Maxwell's equations. I wanted to check on a real machine what the assignment explains on paper.

How it is built

  • Two neodymium magnets that create the magnetic field.
  • Three coils placed between the magnets.
  • A commutator that reverses the current to keep it turning.
  • Supports and parts designed in SOLIDWORKS and 3D-printed.

Process

I first tested the principle with a single coil and, once it worked, assembled all three coils and the commutator until the complete motor was running.
